Kevin, ~30 s. Two delays add up: the head has to move, then each frame goes through camera, encoder, network and decoder. We measure it with a QR code that shows the time and is filmed by Reachy: 233 ms, very steady. A quick head turn would leave the picture over 20° off — that is what makes people sick.

Kevin, ~40 s. Top: video glued to your head — when you turn, the old frame shows the old direction and the room swims.
