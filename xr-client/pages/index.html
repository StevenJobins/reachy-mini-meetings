How it works

Three things work together: the robot on the meeting table, a laptop next to it, and your headset wherever you are.

1

Reachy sits at the table

A Reachy Mini is your place in the room. Its camera and microphones are your eyes and ears; its head and antennas show the others what you do.

2

The laptop does the work

The Mac app next to the robot transcribes and translates what is said, writes the meeting notes and lets your headset in with a pairing code.

3

You put on the headset

You see the room, with a speech bubble above whoever speaks. Everything is worked with one gesture, a pinch of thumb and index finger.

What you get

Built for the person who is not in the room, so that they follow the meeting as well as those who are.

Speech bubbles in your language

What is said appears above the speaker, translated into German, English, French, Italian or Spanish.

Translate me

Speak your own language. Reachy says your sentences in the language of the room, or lets your own voice through.

A body in the room

Reachy follows your head. Raise a hand and it waves for the word; a nod, a head shake or a thumbs-up shows on its antennas.

Meeting notes, written for you

A running summary, who does what by when, and the next steps. One click in the Mac app opens them as a mail draft.

Shared screen

The room shares a screen, an app or a single window from the laptop, and it hangs next to the picture in your headset.

From the same Wi-Fi or from home

Pair the headset once with a six-digit code. One switch in the Mac app opens the meeting to headsets on other networks.

Set up in ten minutes

Once per laptop and headset. After that, opening both apps is all it takes.

  1. Connect Reachy Mini to the MacPlug it in by USB and install Reachy Mini Control from Pollen Robotics.
  2. Open Reachy Meetings on the MacDrag it to Applications and open it. The first time, a progress bar shows while it fetches what it needs (about 3.5 GB, a few minutes). macOS asks once for the microphone, the camera and the local network.
  3. Install the app on the headsetUntil it is in the store: switch on developer mode on the headset, connect it to a computer and run adb install ReachyMeetings.apk.
  4. Pair the headsetOpen the app in the headset and type the six-digit code the Mac app shows. The headset is remembered from then on.
  5. Take the tourThe first start shows every gesture and lets you practise it, while Reachy wakes up and looks around the room.

Questions

The short answers. The details are in the repository.

Where does what is said in the room go?

Speech is turned into text on the Mac itself. The text then goes through our service to DeepL for the translation and to Google Gemini for the meeting notes; our service keeps none of it, only how many minutes each installation used. Video and sound travel from the laptop straight to the headset.

Does it work without a Reachy Mini?

No. The robot is the camera, the microphones and your presence in the room. Without it the apps have nothing to connect to.

Which headsets work?

The app is built and tested on the Samsung Galaxy XR. It needs hand tracking; other Android headsets are untested.

The headset does not connect. What now?

The first time, the headset and the Mac must be on the same Wi-Fi, and the six-digit code from the Mac app is typed into the headset once. A headset that was used with another laptop keeps looking for that one: clear the app's data in the headset's settings, open it again and type the code.

Can several people join through one robot?

One person at a time sees through the robot and turns its head. Several headsets can be paired with the same laptop.

How good is the translation live?

A sentence appears about a second and a half after it was said. Names and technical terms can come out wrong, as with any live transcription.
